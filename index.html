Games

Small browser games and toys. No installs, no build step — each one is a single page that runs straight in the browser.

🎲

3D Dice Roller

Physics-driven dice in d4 through d20. Pick how many, choose numbers or dots, set your colours, then tap to throw.

Sling Puck

The wooden board game: sling your pucks through the gap in the middle. No turns — both sides fire at once, and the first empty half wins.

2048

Slide, merge, and keep the board alive. Boards from 3×3 to 6×6, undo up to twelve moves, best score kept per size.

Tetris

Seven shapes into ten columns. Wall kicks, hold, ghost piece, T-spins and a level that keeps climbing.

Sudoku

Fresh puzzles generated in the browser, graded easy to expert by the techniques they actually need. Pencil marks, hints, best times.

Connect Four

Drop discs down seven columns and get four in a line. Against the computer at three strengths, or two players on one screen.
